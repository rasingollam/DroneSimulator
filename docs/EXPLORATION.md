# World exploration

F6 opens the world explorer while disarmed. Use Up/Down to browse all eighteen starts across two pages, or click a visible row, then Enter to launch. The connected 8 km square landscape keeps its 3.8 km radial flight boundary. R returns to the selected start.

A dry side entrance leads to the camp behind the main waterfall. Fly around the water curtain to reach it; water contact still ends the flight. Natural terrain, structures and physical openings share the same world.

## Optional scenic lines

Press G to cycle these lines; the seventh press hides the guide. F3's instrument map also shows the selected trail.

| Trail | Suggested start |
| --- | --- |
| Summit to lake | High summit |
| Forest and slot canyon | Pine saddle |
| Braided river line | Braided river |
| Coastal cliff traverse | Coastal lookout |
| Dam to quarry | Dam overlook |
| Alpine bowl dive | High summit or Alpine bowl |

Follow the line with your sticks. Take lower, tighter or wider alternatives as you build confidence. The guide is optional and has no checkpoint timer.

## Record and revisit a flight

1. Choose a start and drone, then press Y. You can prepare recording before picking up the phone.
2. Arm and fly. The PC samples the aircraft's position, rotation and simulation time.
3. Press Y to save, or disarm after landing. A crash also ends the recording. A very short recording keeps the previous saved flight.
4. Press P to show the saved drone ghost. Return to the original start, arm and chase it. R restarts the replay clock; P hides it. The ghost loops at the end and respects simulation pause.
5. Try F4's other airframes against the same saved run. The ghost retains the recorded aircraft's size.

There is one saved flight slot. A new completed recording replaces it. Replay uses its recorded world coordinates, so selecting a different F6 start does not move the saved route. Flights save beside `user://exploration.cfg` as `exploration-flight.json`; tests use isolated files.

## Discoveries and landing spots

Approach a landmark to discover it. Hidden camps, the wreck, cave passages, ruins and lookout structures reward exploration. F6 shows your counts. Each place is recorded once.

O optionally marks landing spots, including a lighthouse roof, timber bridge, lookout decks, chimney, dam crest, lake island and dock. Settle upright on the target, disarm, and remain below 0.15 m/s for two seconds to record the landing. A fly-through does not count.

## Atmosphere and sound

F7 cycles morning, golden hour and valley mist. F10 cycles summer, autumn grasses/crops and winter highlands; the season is remembered. Moving cloud shadows and vegetation variation work across terrain detail levels. Conifers remain predominantly green, with a winter frost tint.

Waterfall, coast and bird ambience use spatial audio. Headphones help locate sounds before seeing their source. M mutes both ambience and motors; pausing also pauses ambience. Environmental sounds are generated by the project, with no additional downloads.

F8 changes graphics quality. The OpenGL launcher uses simpler terrain/water shaders and fewer crop cards for older hardware. Stationary structures, paddy water and track details use shared instanced meshes; original collision remains in place. Actual performance varies with hardware and viewing direction.
